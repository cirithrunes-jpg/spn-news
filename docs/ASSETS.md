# Assets da proposta visual

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, sem chave de API externa. Otimizados para WebP, preservando o conteúdo das imagens. Os PNGs originais continuam no acervo local de imagens geradas. Arquivos finais usados pelo site:

- `public/brand/masthead.webp` — cabeçalho ilustrado inspirado na referência visual enviada pelo usuário.
- `public/brand/hero-space.webp` — cena fictícia de aventura espacial.
- `public/brand/editorial-triptych.webp` — composição fictícia de série, cidade de game e música; somente os dois primeiros painéis são usados após a inclusão de fotografias reais licenciadas.

## Prompt: masthead

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: a wide masthead banner for the actual SPN News website, approximately 3:1 landscape. Reference image is the user's desired website design, use only its TOP masthead as style reference. Create a standalone high quality banner matching that masthead: energetic black/navy comic book collage with red and orange diagonal accents, a large glossy round yellow retro television mascot with two antennas and a red screen bearing a white play triangle on the left, then very bold italic white 'SPN' followed by italic orange 'NEWS'. Under the wordmark exact readable small white slogan 'O MUNDO POP LEVADO A SÉRIO. MAIS OU MENOS.'. Right area yellow comic strip with smaller TV mascot and exact text 'NOTÍCIA, MEMES E MUITO MAIS!'. No navigation, no news, no website screenshot, no buttons, just finished banner artwork. Crop tightly like a website masthead, lettering centrally vertically, large legible logo. Preserve yellow TV identity and comic collage energy of reference.

## Prompt: hero

Create a cinematic, photorealistic landscape artwork for a fictional pop culture website hero, 3:2 composition. Two adult space explorers in a spacecraft cockpit, one adult woman with short dark hair in an orange flight jacket and one adult man with wavy hair in a navy jacket. Both look with wonder toward a bright planet through the window. Faces on the right two thirds; left third mostly dark cockpit so a webpage can overlay text. Warm amber light, vivid rich colors, film still composition and shallow depth of field. No weapons, no danger, no real people, no actual movie characters, no text, no logos, no watermark, no interface. A beautiful fictional science-fiction adventure illustration.

## Prompt: triptych

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one cohesive three-panel cinematic triptych artwork, landscape 3:1, three equal-width side-by-side panels separated by a crisp boundary. No text or UI. Left panel: fictional adult female fantasy adventurer in a dark forest with golden light and dramatic wind, photographic TV drama style. Middle panel: colorful cinematic video-game illustration of a neon sunset tropical city with palm trees, purple and orange, no people, no existing game logos. Right panel: fictional adult female pop singer with dark hair and blue highlights, on a blue-lit stage, photographic concert style. All imagery is invented for a clearly marked demonstration portal, not actual releases or real people. Rich entertainment editorial photography, strong composition, no logos, words, watermark, borders, or captions. The three individual panels form ONE finished triptych asset, each center subject composed for a website thumbnail with dark lower area for text overlays.
